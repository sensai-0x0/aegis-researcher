  AFFECTS, CLASSIFIED_AS, MITIGATED_BY, REVIEWED_BY, DERIVED_FROM.
- **Required provenance fields:** `record_id`, `created_at`, `created_by_or_agent_run`,
  `mission_id`, `source_artifact_ids`, `schema_version`.
- **Edge attributes:** confidence (0–1, method + author), independent_observation_count,
  reproducibility (0–1 + experiment refs), source_quality, last_verified_at, impact
  (severity is never conflated with confidence).
- **Graph rules:** no secrets/credentials/private keys in Memgraph; raw files live in
  the artifact store, graph holds references/hashes only; model-generated statements
  are not independent evidence; shared model/context is not independent corroboration;
  confidence/severity/recency/reproducibility are distinct dimensions; every confirmed
  finding links to supporting evidence and a review record.
- Memgraph-specific work items found at curriculum lines ~416 (P01: missions/runs/
